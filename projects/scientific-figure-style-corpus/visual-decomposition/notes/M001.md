## Layout and reading logic
Two large conceptual domains sit diagonally across the canvas with an open middle exchange zone. Numbered markers create reading order without hard panel borders.

## Rendering
Soft flat editorial shapes with simplified biological domains and compact internal modules rather than literal whole-cell anatomy.

## Connector grammar
Solid arrows show local process flow. Dotted connectors show cross-domain exchange or influence.

## Palette and hierarchy
Pale green and pale yellow-peach separate the partners; the shared environment remains light. More saturated accents carry emphasis.

## Reusable elements
Two focal domains around one shared interface; dotted cross-partner connectors versus solid local arrows; numbered stages; open central exchange zone.

## Best use
Two-partner exchange, extracellular coupling, or other interaction schematics where only a few resources cross between organisms.

## Avoid
Avoid too many crossing dotted connectors or too many exchange labels. Keep the center visually open.
