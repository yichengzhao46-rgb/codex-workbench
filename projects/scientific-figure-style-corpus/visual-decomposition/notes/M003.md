# M003

## 1. Figure identity
- Canonical ID: M003
- Legacy ID: S007
- Source type: local curated asset
- Visual class: mechanism + evidence dashboard

## 2. Functional role
The figure uses one dominant mechanistic schematic to explain the biological process, then places compact expression/validation panels beside it. Its job is not only to show a pathway, but to make the supporting evidence visibly subordinate to the mechanistic core.

## 3. Layout and reading logic
The left half is the visual anchor: a large cell-centered Sb(V)/Sb(III) mechanism with photoelectron/electron inputs. The right half is a modular evidence dashboard organized into grouped functional categories and small validation plots. Reading order is left-to-right: mechanism first, evidence second.

## 4. Cell / object rendering
The cell is a pale-blue, soft-edged flat silhouette. Intracellular modules are embedded directly into the cell rather than separated into organelle-like compartments. Small bacterial silhouettes outside the cell provide context without competing with the main mechanism.

## 5. Arrow grammar
Electron transfer is differentiated through warm yellow/orange electron cues and explicit e− symbols. Biochemical/process arrows remain darker and more conventional. This separation prevents electron flow from being confused with generic causal arrows.

## 6. Palette / outline / text hierarchy
Cool cyan/blue establishes the biological scaffold; yellow marks electron/photoelectron semantics; orange/green/blue distinguish functional evidence classes. Panel letters, grouped headers and the large left-hand mechanism establish hierarchy despite high information density.

## 7. Information density and evidence style
Density is high, but asymmetric: the mechanism is visually large and conceptually simple, while supporting evidence is compressed into repeated small modules. This is more readable than giving each data panel equal visual weight.

## 8. Best reusable elements
- asymmetric mechanism + evidence split;
- grouped functional evidence headers;
- explicit electron color semantics;
- one dominant conceptual panel paired with several compact validation modules.

## 9. Suitable future use cases in Bath–RP project
Useful for a final mechanism figure where a Bath/RP conceptual model is accompanied by transcriptomic module summaries, qPCR/physiology support, or a few compact validation readouts.

## 10. Weaknesses / what not to imitate
Do not reproduce the tiny pathway labels or use too many equally saturated evidence colors. The right-side dashboard must remain readable at final publication size. The figure is strongest as a composition grammar, not as a template for exact pathway density.
