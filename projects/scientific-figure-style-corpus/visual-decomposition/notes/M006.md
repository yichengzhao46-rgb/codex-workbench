## 1. Figure identity
- Canonical ID: M006
- Legacy ID: S045

## 2. Functional role
This figure places a time-series result directly above a compact mechanism that explains the observed temporal behavior. Its strength is the short distance between empirical evidence and interpretation.

## 3. Layout and reading logic
The upper panel contains the quantitative trend. The lower panel compresses the proposed mechanism into a circular charge/discharge loop coupling a methane oxidizer, an Fe redox-storage module and a CO2 reducer. Reading order is top evidence → bottom interpretation.

## 4. Cell / object rendering
Organisms are simplified colored icons rather than detailed cells. The central redox medium is represented with block/mineral/battery-like states, making the storage function immediately legible.

## 5. Arrow grammar
Red circular arrows encode charging/discharging electron movement. Black arrows denote material transformation or process direction. The loop structure itself carries the causal story, so few arrows are needed.

## 6. Palette / outline / text hierarchy
The methane-oxidizer side is cool blue, the CO2-reducer side is warm brown/orange, the mineral/storage core is dark neutral, and electron movement is red. This creates a clear three-part semantic palette.

## 7. Information density and evidence style
Density is medium. The plot uses standard quantitative conventions, while the lower schematic is deliberately sparse. The figure demonstrates that a mechanism panel does not need to repeat all the data once the evidence panel has established the trend.

## 8. Best reusable elements
- data-to-mechanism vertical stack;
- circular redox/battery loop;
- paired organism endpoints around a central mediator;
- one accent color reserved for charging/discharging.

## 9. Suitable future use cases in Bath–RP project
Useful when a single major experimental trend is followed immediately by a mechanistic interpretation: for example high-O2 perturbation, donor-pool suppression, redox-storage behavior, or a compact summary of condition-dependent metabolic coupling.

## 10. Weaknesses / what not to imitate
Do not let the lower schematic imply greater certainty than the data support. Avoid decorative battery imagery if redox storage is not central. Keep the interpretation panel compact so the evidence retains primacy.
