# Reference 001

## Composition
Two large conceptual domains sit diagonally across the canvas with an open middle exchange zone. Three numbered markers provide reading order without hard panel borders.

## Rendering
Soft flat editorial shapes, pale semantic zones, and compact internal pathway modules. The biological domains are conceptual rather than literal whole-cell drawings.

## Connector grammar
Solid arrows show local process flow. Dotted arrows show cross-domain exchange or influence.

## Palette and hierarchy
Pale green and pale yellow-peach separate the two partners; the shared environment remains light and open. Redox accents are more saturated than the background.

## Reusable elements
- two focal domains around one shared interface;
- dotted inter-partner connectors versus solid local arrows;
- numbered mechanism stages;
- open central exchange zone.

## Suitable use
Useful for two-partner exchange diagrams where both partners need clear separation while only a few extracellular resources cross the shared space.

## Avoid
Avoid too many crossing dotted connectors or too many exchange labels. Keep the center visually open.
