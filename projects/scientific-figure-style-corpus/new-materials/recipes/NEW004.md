# NEW004 — Road-to-river-to-people environmental fate chain

## Drawing idea
Embed a source-to-receptor causal chain inside one environmental scene.

## Composition
Road/source in lower-left → runoff/air transport → river corridor → soil/crop/water accumulation → ecological/human exposure → distributed mitigation interventions.

## Hierarchy
Source → transport/transformation → environmental media → receptors → mitigation.

## Object rendering
Vector landscape with simplified infrastructure and ecological icons; small process callouts only where useful.

## Palette
Muted earth/water background; blue transport arrows; red dissolved/transformation arrows; focal risk points in restrained warm accents.

## Connector grammar
Use a small legend to define arrow/process classes once. Place interventions at their real process location instead of in a separate “solutions” box.

## Whitespace
Use river corridors and sky as label zones.

## Information density
Medium-high.

## Reusable rules
- Arrange by where the science happens, not by concept category.
- A transport corridor can serve simultaneously as process path and visual spine.
- Legends prevent repeated explanatory text.

## Best use
Pollutant fate, exposure pathways, aerosol-to-environment stories, wetland contaminant transport.

## Avoid transfer
Too many exposure labels, arrows crossing physical boundaries without reason, decorative city detail.

## Prompt logic
Lock the source-to-receptor chain first, then build a scene around it; add transformation hotspots and management only after the causal route is readable.
