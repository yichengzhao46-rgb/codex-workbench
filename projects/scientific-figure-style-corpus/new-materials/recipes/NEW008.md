# NEW008 — CAR-T macro-to-micro mechanism

## Drawing idea
Use two scales to answer two questions: where/what happens, then how it happens.

## Composition
Upper macro tissue/context scene; lower enlarged cell–cell interface.

## Hierarchy
1. Spatial/biological context.
2. Local interaction.
3. Molecular mechanism.

## Object rendering
Consistent editorial cell identities across scales; simplified membranes and receptors at micro scale; no unrelated intracellular detail.

## Palette
Persistent identity colors across scales, high-lightness backgrounds, restrained mechanism accents.

## Connector grammar
One explicit macro-to-micro transition; local micro arrows indicate binding/release/signaling.

## Whitespace
Strong separation between macro and micro panels.

## Information density
Medium.

## Reusable rules
- Preserve object identity across magnification.
- Use the macro view for “where/what”; reserve molecular detail for “how”.
- A local interface can be enlarged without redrawing the whole system.

## Best use
Bath–RP coculture → diffusible exchange zone → recipient response; material–cell interface; membrane EET/EEU.

## Avoid transfer
Repeating the same information at both scales; over-populating the microscopic interface; implying unsupported molecular specificity.

## Prompt logic
Lock identity colors and macro scene first; choose one scientifically important interface for magnification; constrain the lower panel to only mechanism-bearing elements.
