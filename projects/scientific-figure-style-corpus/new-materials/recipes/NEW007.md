# NEW007 — Wastewater treatment and reuse process schematic

## Drawing idea
Turn a laboratory/engineering workflow into a publication-grade process spine rather than a PowerPoint flowchart.

## Composition
One left-to-right treatment spine built from standardized equipment modules; small resource-recovery branches beneath; circular/compact mechanism zooms attached to selected stages.

## Hierarchy
Main process spine → secondary recovery outputs → local mechanism insets.

## Object rendering
Simple editorial equipment modules, light 2.5D only, repeated scale and line weight.

## Palette
White canvas, pale blue/grey equipment, one warm accent family for active chemistry, minimal background blocks.

## Connector grammar
One dominant horizontal flow; short local branches; zoom leaders only where a stage needs mechanistic explanation.

## Whitespace
High around the spine; insets live in unused regions rather than competing with the process.

## Information density
Medium.

## Reusable rules
- Replace rectangular “step boxes” with standardized scientific objects.
- The main line should remain readable without the insets.
- Secondary outputs branch off without bending the primary route.

## Best use
Experimental setup, cultivation workflow, reactor train, process schematic, Bath–RP inoculum→allocation→incubation figure.

## Avoid transfer
Large 1/2/3 panel boxes, oversized decorative equipment, many curved arrows, duplicate labels.

## Prompt logic
First define the process spine and stage spacing; then choose one object per stage; only after that add branches/insets and semantic colors.
