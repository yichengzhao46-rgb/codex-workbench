# Stage 1.5 harvest workflow (public OA wave 1)

This workflow refines the scientific figure style corpus beyond the validated 100-image raw corpus.

## Scope

Only use **2024–2026** articles that are:

- open access;
- redistribution-compatible for public mirroring (prefer CC BY / CC0);
- relevant to the project’s key visual domains.

## Key visual domains

- integrated mechanism;
- microbial interaction / cross-feeding;
- electron transfer / redox mediators;
- material–microbe or semiconductor–microbe interface;
- environmental gradients / oxic–anoxic layering.

## Per-paper figure cap

- Normal: **1 figure**
- Maximum: **2 figures**, only when the second one adds a clearly different and high-transfer layout

## Strong exclusion rules

Do not harvest routine figures dominated by:

- bar charts, line plots, scatter plots;
- PCA, volcano plots, routine heatmaps;
- microscopy-only panels;
- purely data-driven layouts with low style-transfer value.

## Screening procedure

For each source article:

1. confirm the article and figure-level redistribution rights;
2. inspect all figures visually;
3. reject routine quantitative and microscopy-only figures;
4. shortlist at most 1–2 figures;
5. assign `primary_purpose`, `style_family`, `layout_archetype`, and A/B/C/exclude tier;
6. record why the figure transfers to Bath–RP, Bath–Se, GAC/DIET, or environmental-gradient tasks;
7. only then mirror the image into a Stage 1.5 public asset directory.

## Output expectation

Each accepted figure should receive:

- figure locator;
- rights confirmation;
- primary purpose;
- style family;
- layout archetype;
- style-learning tier (A/B/C/exclude);
- Bath–RP transfer notes.

## Safety and provenance

Do not place figures with CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, CC BY-NC-ND, unclear reuse terms, or excluded third-party artwork in the public asset directory. Record those papers as metadata/private-reference-only instead.
