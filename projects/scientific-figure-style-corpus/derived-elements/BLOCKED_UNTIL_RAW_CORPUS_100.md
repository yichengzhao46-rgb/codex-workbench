# Element extraction gate

Element-library expansion is intentionally **blocked** until the raw corpus contains at least 100 legally mirrored, provenance-checked, checksum-verified, visually inspected source images.

The existing element/style schema may remain for planning, but no new canonical element family should be promoted from the current 20-record pilot.

## Unblock conditions

See `../RAW_CORPUS_POLICY.md`. In short:

- mirrored image count >= 100;
- at least 60 unique articles;
- all four target journal families represented;
- rights and third-party-material checks complete for every counted asset;
- duplicate/perceptual-duplicate review complete;
- functional diversity targets substantially satisfied.

Only after this gate passes should the project begin systematic extraction of recurring:

- microbial cell visual archetypes;
- material/mineral/GAC/electrode archetypes;
- molecule/gas/metabolite tokens;
- electron and redox symbols;
- arrow/connector semantics;
- gradients and environmental layers;
- zoom/callout structures;
- typography and label hierarchy;
- reusable composition patterns.
